This satellite conference focuses on algorithmic research addressing privacy and security challenges in biomedical research. The program includes keynote presentations by renowned experts in the field, alongside contributed talks and poster presentations. All submissions will undergo a rigorous peer-review process.

Venue

RECOMB-Privacy 2026 and other RECOMB meetings will be held at HELEXPO in Thessaloniki, Greece. All registrations will be coordinated by the local RECOMB organization. See the main RECOMB website for more information.
